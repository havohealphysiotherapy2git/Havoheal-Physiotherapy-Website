# Ethical backlink plan

A realistic, white-hat plan for earning links to
`havohealphysiotherapy.co.uk`.

---

## What this plan will never do

These are not stylistic preferences. Each one risks a Google manual action, and
several would also breach advertising standards for a healthcare business.

- ❌ Buying links, or paying for "guest posts" on link-selling sites
- ❌ Automated link building, link farms or private blog networks (PBNs)
- ❌ Reciprocal link schemes ("link to us and we'll link to you")
- ❌ Bulk directory submissions to irrelevant sites
- ❌ Comment, forum or profile spam
- ❌ Fake reviews, fake testimonials or fabricated case studies
- ❌ Hidden links, cloaked pages or doorway pages
- ❌ Any healthcare directory claiming a registration the business does not hold

**Expected pace:** a legitimate local health business might earn 2–5 quality
links a month once outreach is running. That is normal and it compounds. Anyone
promising 100 links a month is selling you a penalty.

## Priority 1 — Local business and membership organisations

Genuine memberships that come with a directory listing.

| Target | How | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Greater Birmingham Chambers of Commerce** | Paid membership includes a member directory listing | Low effort, real value |
| **Federation of Small Businesses** | Membership includes a listing | Low |
| **Birmingham City Council business directory** | Free listing for local businesses | Low |
| **West Midlands Growth Company** | Business support directories | Medium |
| **Local BID (Business Improvement District) sites** | Where you operate | Medium |

These links are relevant, local and permanent — the profile Google trusts most.

---

## Priority 2 — Community organisations

The highest-quality local links come from being genuinely useful.

| Target | The offer |
| --- | --- |
| **Running clubs** (Birmingham Running Athletics & Triathlon Club, Bournville Harriers, Kings Heath Running Club) | A free 30-minute talk on warm-up routines and managing training load |
| **parkrun** (Cannon Hill, Sutton Park, Perry Hall, Walsall Arboretum) | Volunteer, sponsor a run, offer a pre-run mobility session |
| **Amateur football and rugby clubs** | Pitch-side support at a tournament, or an educational session for coaches |
| **Cycling clubs** | A session on bike-fit-related discomfort and posture |
| **Community centres and libraries** | A free "movement for over-60s" talk |
| **Local charities** | Support a Birmingham charity event on its own merits |

Most publish a partners, sponsors or news page. The link follows the
contribution, and the relationship is worth more than the link.

---

## Priority 3 — Gyms, studios and health partners

Mutual referral relationships that make sense for both sides.

- Independent gyms across Birmingham, Solihull, Sutton Coldfield and the Black
  Country
- Yoga and Pilates studios
- CrossFit boxes
- Sports massage therapists
- Personal trainers with their own sites

The offer: you become the person they refer members to when something needs more
than a training tweak; they get a professional partner. A "partners" page link
usually follows naturally.

> Keep it clean: no payment for referrals, and no arrangement that would breach
> professional standards.

---

## Priority 4 — Verified healthcare directories

Only where eligibility is genuinely met. See
[`local-seo-guide.md`](local-seo-guide.md#4-healthcare-and-physiotherapy-directories).

Most require HCPC registration or CSP membership. **Do not apply until those are
in place** — and when they are, the CSP "Find a Physio" listing is likely the
single most valuable link available to this business.

---

## Priority 5 — Original resources that earn links

Links follow from resources people want to reference. Realistic candidates:

1. **"Physiotherapy pricing in Birmingham: what you actually pay"** — a
   transparent, well-researched comparison of how physiotherapy is priced
   locally. Transparent pricing content gets cited because most providers hide
   theirs.
2. **"A desk-worker's guide to movement breaks"** — a printable one-page PDF that
   local employers can share with staff. Employers link to useful things.
3. **"Questions to ask before booking physiotherapy"** — a genuinely
   consumer-first checklist, including questions that are awkward for providers
   to answer.
4. **"Understanding the difference between an acknowledgement and a confirmed
   booking"** — a short, honest explainer. Niche, but it is exactly the kind of
   clarity that gets shared.

Write these because they are useful. The links are a by-product.

## Priority 6 — Digital PR and local media

| Outlet | Angle |
| --- | --- |
| **Birmingham Live / Birmingham Mail** | Expert comment on a seasonal story (desk posture and hybrid working; injury prevention before a local race) |
| **Birmingham World** | Local health and wellbeing |
| **BBC Radio WM** | Expert contributor on a health segment |
| **Local district papers** (Solihull Observer, Express & Star) | Community-level stories |
| **University of Birmingham / Aston student media** | Student sport injury prevention |

Journalists want a quotable expert who replies quickly, not a press release.
Register on **#JournoRequest** (X/Twitter) and **ResponseSource** and answer only
the requests you can genuinely speak to.

> Do not comment as a clinical expert unless the person quoted actually holds the
> relevant qualifications. Attribute quotes to a named, qualified person.

## Priority 7 — Unlinked brand mentions

As the business grows, people will mention "Havoheal" without linking.

1. Set a Google Alert for `"Havoheal"` and `"Havoheal Physiotherapy UK LTD"`.
2. Search monthly: `"Havoheal" -site:havohealphysiotherapy.co.uk`
3. When you find a mention without a link, send the template below.

This is the highest-conversion outreach there is — they already wrote about you.

---

## Priority 8 — Broken-link outreach

When a Birmingham health resource links to a physiotherapy page that no longer
exists, and you have something genuinely equivalent:

1. Find local resource pages: `site:*.bham.ac.uk physiotherapy resources`,
   `"Birmingham" "physiotherapy" inurl:links`, club and community "useful links"
   pages.
2. Check them with a broken-link checker.
3. Only get in touch if you have a genuinely comparable replacement. Suggesting
   your homepage as a replacement for a dead NHS guide is spam.

---

## Priority 9 — Competitor backlink gap research

Use this to find **opportunities**, never to copy a spam profile.

1. Pick 3–5 established Birmingham physiotherapy providers.
2. Run their domains through Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz (free tiers are enough at
   this scale).
3. Filter for links you could legitimately earn: local directories, club
   sponsorships, chamber memberships, community pages, genuine press.
4. Ignore anything that looks bought, syndicated or PBN-shaped. If you cannot see
   why the link exists, do not chase it.

## Measuring it

Track monthly in a simple spreadsheet:

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Target site | Domain |
| Type | Directory / community / partner / PR / resource |
| Contact and date | Who and when |
| Status | Not started / sent / replied / live / declined |
| Live URL | The actual link |
| Follow / nofollow | Both have value; nofollow still drives referrals |
| Referral visits | If analytics is enabled |
| Enquiries | The only number that really matters |

Review quarterly. If a channel produces links but no enquiries, spend the time
elsewhere — a link that never sends a patient is worth less than a relationship
that does.

---

## Red flags in anyone's pitch

If an agency or freelancer offers any of the following, decline:

- A guaranteed number of links per month
- Guaranteed first-page rankings
- "High DA links" with no named sites
- Links from "our private network"
- Anything requiring access to your site to insert links
- Prices that seem too good — they are

A genuine local SEO consultant will talk about your Google Business Profile,
reviews, local partnerships and content quality long before they mention links.
